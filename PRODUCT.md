# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fish farm owners, managers, and field workers in rural Bangladesh. Primary usage is on entry-level Android smartphones in outdoor conditions (bright daylight, humid/wet hands, intermittent connectivity). Managers and owners are typically over 50 years old. Field workers may have low literacy and limited digital experience. The job to be done is managing multi-pond fisheries operations: tracking stock, recording sales, logging feed and medicine, monitoring expenses, and coordinating with partners.

## Product Purpose

Deshi Fishery is a fisheries management platform that helps Bangladeshi fish farmers run their operations digitally. It replaces paper ledgers and memory-based tracking with a simple, reliable app that works offline and syncs when connected. Success looks like a farm owner or manager who can check stock levels, record a sale, or review expenses in under 30 seconds without needing help.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, traditional, trustworthy.

- **Friendly / approachable**: Warm tone, no corporate jargon, welcoming to first-time smartphone users.
- **Traditional / rooted in rural Bangladesh**: Respects the user's context; visuals and language feel local, not imported.
- **Clear and trustworthy**: Financial data must feel reliable and unambiguous; no hidden complexity.

## Anti-references

- Generic government form patterns: dense tables, tiny fields, no visual hierarchy, bureaucratic language.
- Overly edgy or sci-fi aesthetics: neon accents, dark-mode-as-default-gimmick, futuristic icons, gradients-for-everything.
- Cluttered dashboards: information overload, too many charts, competing call-to-action buttons, nested cards.
- Corporate SaaS minimalism: sterile whites, abstract illustrations, impersonal copy.
- Complex onboarding: multi-step tutorials, tooltips everywhere, forced feature tours.

## Design Principles

1. **The field comes first**: Design for bright sunlight, wet hands, and shaky connectivity before designing for a desk. Every screen must be usable one-handed in direct daylight.
2. **Trust through clarity**: Financial and stock data must be instantly readable. No ambiguity in numbers, status, or actions. If a user hesitates, the design has failed.
3. **Respect the user's context**: Language, numerals, currency, and visual cues must feel native to rural Bangladesh. Nothing should feel like it was designed for a different country and translated.
4. **Progressive disclosure for low literacy**: Surface the essential action or number first; details and explanations are one tap away, never in the way.
5. **Offline is not an error state**: The app must feel fully functional without a connection. Sync status is informative, not alarming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** compliance target.
- **Low vision**: High contrast mode available for sunlight readability; large touch targets (56x56dp minimum); resizable text respecting system font size.
- **Low literacy**: Minimal text per screen; icons paired with labels; progressive disclosure of complex information; Bengali language support with Bengali numerals.
- **Motor**: Large tap targets, generous spacing between interactive elements, forgiving hit areas.
- **Cognitive**: Consistent patterns, predictable navigation, clear status feedback, no time limits on forms.
- **Dark mode**: Supported for nighttime use, but light mode is the default (better for daylight readability).
